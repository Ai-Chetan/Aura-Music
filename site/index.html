A music app that treats your library like a collection, not a feed.

Most players keep your music on their servers, behind an account, arranged by whatever they would like you to hear next. Stop paying and the library stops being yours.

Aura works the other way round. The files, the tags, the play history and the recommendations all sit on your phone, where they keep working with no signal and no sign-in.

  • 0 ads, ever

Singles, Shorts and playlists of up to 50 tracks save in the background, keeping every song in its original quality. One failed track is reported on its own and never takes the rest down with it.

Step 04 · Library

Tags, filters and sorting that keep up.

Give a track as many tags as it deserves, then mix and match them to filter, search by title or artist, and sort six different ways. Downloads and saved tracks sit side by side.

Step 05 · Radio

A radio that learns you, on your phone.

Plays, replays and skips teach Aura what you like — all privately on your phone. Shuffle plays only what you picked, and Spice up keeps the music going after any playlist ends — you're always in control.

Built for people who keep things.

Six parts of Aura you will use every week — the ones that decide whether a library is a pleasure or a chore.

Getting started 01

Guided from the first tap.

A skippable tour runs over the real interface with floating coach tips, then offers a handful of starter tracks that download in the background while you look around.

Downloads 02

A queue you can watch.

The Downloading card shows overall progress and a row per song, with cancel and a retry report for anything that failed. Imports keep running when you leave the screen.

Saved 03

Saved, without the download.

Save a track without downloading and it still starts almost instantly with the same tags, filters and sorting as the songs on your phone.

Playback 04

Queue, shuffle, spice.

Play next, reorder, shuffle and repeat across a real queue. Shuffle play respects whatever you have filtered, and Spice up quietly lines up fresh picks behind the playlist you are on.

Your metadata 05

Every track, filed your way.

Fix a title, swap the artwork, correct an artist or add another tag. One song can wear many tags, so it can sit in as many corners of your library as it belongs to.

Portability 06

Back up. Move on. Restore.

Save a backup file — your whole library or just the parts you choose — share it anywhere, then bring everything back when you switch phones, tags included.

Questions, answered plainly.

The short version of what Aura is: a lightweight, free offline music player for Android with no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

Is Aura an offline music player?

Yes. Aura is designed around locally stored audio and offline playback. Songs you save live on your phone and keep playing with no signal and no sign-in — see why Aura works offline.

Is Aura available for Android?

Yes. Aura is an Android music player (Android 8 and newer) distributed as a free app file (APK) on GitHub Releases. There is no account, no backend and no cloud library.

Does Aura have ads, accounts or tracking?

No. Aura ships with zero ads, zero tracking, and nothing to sign up for. Your files, tags and listening history never leave the device — read the privacy notes.

How does Aura organize music with tags?

Every track can carry many tags instead of living in one playlist. Mix and match tags to filter, search by title or artist, and sort six ways. The features tour shows the library, queue and radio in action.

Does Aura support lock-screen controls and queues?

Yes. You get notification and lock-screen controls, plus a real queue with play-next, reorder, shuffle and repeat. Download Aura free and try it.

Is Aura free and open source?

Yes. Aura is completely free — no paywalls, no premium tier — and open source under the MIT licence. Browse the source on GitHub, report issues, or fork it. Contributors are welcome — see the contributing guide to get started. Developed by Ai-Chetan for the open-source community.

Who owns the music in Aura?

Aura doesn't own any songs and claims no rights over them. All music, album art, and names belong to their respective artists, labels, and rights holders — YouTube™ is a trademark of Google LLC. Aura is only a private, on-device player with no sharing built in: it simply keeps what you choose on your own phone for offline listening. Please save only content you own or have permission to keep, and respect creators and platform terms.
